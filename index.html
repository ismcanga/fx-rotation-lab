Pair history

Daily: ECB reference-rate line. Weekly, monthly and quarterly: ranges of daily ECB reference observations; left tick = first observation, right tick = last observation.

Aroon

Shows whether recent highs or recent lows are dominating. High Up = a high happened recently; high Down = a low happened recently.

Read: Up high / Down low = recent highs dominate. Reverse = recent lows dominate.

Range position (stochastic)

Shows where the selected granularity's closing reference rate sits inside its recent range. Coarse periods are derived from ECB daily reference observations; these are not intraday market OHLC quotes.

Read: High = near the top of the recent range. Low = near the bottom. High does not automatically mean reversal.

Kaufman efficiency ratio

Shows how cleanly the rate travelled. Near 1 = relatively direct; near 0 = lots of back-and-forth.

Read: Higher = cleaner path. Lower = more zig-zag. Direction comes from the other charts.
