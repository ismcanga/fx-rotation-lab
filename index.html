Indicator reading

These are observations, not trade instructions. Parameters are deliberately visible so they can later be tested rather than tuned silently.

Momentum

Percentage change over the selected lookback: 100 × (Pₜ / Pₜ₋ₖ − 1).

Mean reversion

Rolling z-score: (Pₜ − rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation. Large absolute values flag unusual displacement; they do not prove reversal.

Move journal

Manual record only. Saved in this browser with localStorage; nothing is sent to a server. This is not yet the accounting engine.
